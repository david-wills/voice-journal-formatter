## The ride isn't about the exercise

It's Tuesday, and I just got back from the ride out to the reservoir. I wanted to get this down before I forget it.

The thing about riding in the morning is that it's not actually about the exercise. I tell myself it is, but honestly, if it were about the exercise I'd just go to the gym, which is four blocks away and takes no planning at all.

It's about the fact that nobody can reach me. That's the whole thing. For fifty minutes there's no Slack, no phone. I think that's the only time in the week where I'm actually thinking instead of just reacting to things.

## Why the caching argument won't resolve

That's connected to the thing I've been stuck on at work. We've been going back and forth on the caching layer for three weeks now, and I realized on the ride that the reason we can't decide is that we're not actually disagreeing about caching.

Marcus thinks the problem is latency. I think the problem is cost. Those are just different problems that happen to have overlapping solutions, so of course we keep talking past each other.

I should say that out loud in standup tomorrow instead of bringing another proposal.

I don't know if that's right. It might be that I'm just annoyed and looking for a tidy explanation. But it felt true on the bike.
